When a clogged drain turns into an emergency

Drain cleaning means removing a blockage from a sink, tub, floor drain or sewer line with a cable machine, a hand auger or a water jet, then confirming the line flows. In Louisville, CO, the calls that cannot wait are the ones where wastewater is rising or several fixtures stop draining together.

Our 24/7 emergency line answers at night, on weekends and on holidays, because a basement floor drain does not care what day it is. We give a clear price before work starts.

A slow drain in one bathroom is a different problem from a house where the toilet gurgles when the washer drains. The first is usually a local clog in the trap or branch line. The second points to the main line, and that is where most overnight calls begin.

If the clog keeps returning, a camera inspection shows why, and a stubborn buildup may call for hydro jetting. Homeowners in Lafayette book the same visits.

Drain backing up? Do this while you wait

Every fixture in a house drains into the same pipe, so one backing-up drain is a warning for the others. These steps limit the mess until the plumber arrives.

  • Stop running water everywhere: no flushing, laundry, dishwasher or showers.
  • Skip chemical drain openers. Caustic liquid sitting in a blocked pipe is hard on the pipe and hazardous for whoever has to open it.
  • Lift rugs and boxes away from basement floor drains and the laundry area.
  • Find the sewer cleanout, usually a capped pipe in the basement, garage or beside the foundation, and tell the dispatcher where it is.
  • Keep children and pets out of any standing water that came up from a drain.

What your drains are telling you

The pattern of symptoms narrows the location of the clog before anyone picks up a tool.

One sink or tub is slow

A hair, soap or grease clog in the trap or branch line. A cable or auger clears it from the fixture side.

Several fixtures gurgle or back up together

The blockage is downstream of all of them, in the main line. This is the case that needs attention right away.

Basement floor drain wets when the washer runs

Water has nowhere to go but the lowest opening. Stop laundry and call, because the line is close to full.

The same drain clogs every few weeks

Roots, grease buildup or a sagging section are likely. Cleaning buys time, and a camera shows what keeps causing it.

Who owns the pipe in Louisville

City of Louisville detail sheets for new service connections state that the sewer service line is the property owner's responsibility, including camera inspection and any repair or replacement. The City maintains the mains in the street. Some subdivisions also have gravity underdrains, and those are listed as the owner's responsibility as well.

That split matters on a bad night. If only your house is backing up, the blockage is probably on your side of the connection. If neighbors report the same thing, tell Louisville Public Works as well, because the blockage may be in the main.

Homes in Old Town, which grew around the coal mines that opened in 1877, can have older sewer materials than the subdivisions built after the 1970s. A sewer camera inspection shows what a given house actually has.

Kitchen sink clogs and the disposal

Kitchen lines clog differently. Cooking grease cools and sticks to pipe walls, and food scraps that pass the disposal add to it. If the sink drains slowly with the disposal running, the blockage is often past the disposal, and a garbage disposal repair visit may be unnecessary.

We cable from the cleanout when the sink line is too tight for a hand auger, and we run the sink to confirm it drains before we pack up.
