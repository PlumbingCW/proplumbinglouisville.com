What hydro jetting does that a cable cannot

Hydro jetting sends water through a flexible hose at very high pressure, and the nozzle's rear-facing jets pull it along the pipe while scouring the walls. It removes grease, scale, silt and fine roots across the full diameter, not just a channel down the middle.

A cable machine is the right first tool for a fresh clog. Jetting earns its place when the line has been snaked before, flows again for a few weeks, then slows down. That pattern means the pipe wall is still coated, and the next clog is already forming.

Jetting often follows a camera look at the pipe, and a lighter blockage may only need drain cleaning. We also run these jobs in Broomfield.

Sewer backup: what to do first

A sewer backup is an emergency because the water is contaminated and rising. Our 24/7 line takes these calls at any hour.

  • Stop all water use in the house, including toilets and washing machines.
  • Keep people and pets out of the affected area and avoid touching standing water.
  • Shut off power to a flooded basement only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. Otherwise call the electric utility.
  • Take photos of the water line and damaged items for your insurer.
  • Call, and tell the dispatcher whether the water is coming from a floor drain, toilet or tub.

Signs a jetting visit may be the better call

Plumbers recommend jetting when the symptoms point at the pipe wall, not a single obstruction.

Snaked before, clogged again

The cable opened a path but left the coating. Flow returns, then fades within weeks.

Grease from a restaurant or busy kitchen

Fat hardens into a layer that cables slide through. Jetting strips it off the wall.

Fine roots at several joints

A cutter clears the main root mass. Jetting removes the hair-like roots a blade leaves behind.

Slow drains across the whole house

A uniform slowdown suggests buildup along the main line instead of one blockage.

Why we look at the pipe before we jet

Jetting is not safe on every line. A cracked clay pipe, a collapsed section or an offset joint can be made worse by pressure. For that reason the first step on a repeat-backup call is a camera run, which shows whether the pipe can take a jet and whether the real problem is a defect that no cleaning will cure.

That camera step is also how you learn which part of the line is yours to fix. Louisville's detail sheets list the sewer service line, including inspection and repair, as the property owner's responsibility. If the footage shows a broken section, our sewer line repair page explains the options, including trenchless methods.

For homes in Old Town, where some of the oldest lines in the city run, we are especially careful about pressure. In newer subdivisions with plastic pipe, jetting is usually straightforward.

Commercial kitchens and grease lines

Restaurants and food businesses along Main Street and Front Street produce the grease that most often defeats cable machines. A scheduled jetting visit before a line fails costs far less than an overnight closure. Our commercial plumbing team can set up recurring visits for a building with a known grease problem.
