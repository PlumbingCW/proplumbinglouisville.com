Broomfield's water, and why hardness matters

Broomfield's 2023 water quality report says its supply is Colorado-Big Thompson water from Carter Lake and Glasser Reservoir, treated at the City's plant, with some Denver Water purchased. Hardness across the system ranged from 24 to 140 ppm, or 1.4 to 7.9 grains per gallon.

That is a wide range, so where you live in Broomfield affects how much scale you see. If crust builds on your fixtures or inside a water heater, test your tap and see our page on water softeners and filtration.
