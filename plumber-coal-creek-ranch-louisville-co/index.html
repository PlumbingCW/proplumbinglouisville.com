A neighborhood rebuilding after the Marshall Fire

The City of Louisville's Comprehensive Plan existing conditions appendix calls this area Coal Creek. It borders US 36, once contained a large golf course surrounded by single-family and multifamily housing, and was heavily affected by the Marshall Fire. News coverage uses the name Coal Creek Ranch for the neighborhood beside the Coal Creek Golf Course.

Colorado AvidGolfer reported in January 2022 that Coal Creek Ranch lost 161 homes in the fire. Denver7 reported in December 2025 that new homes dot the streets and a handful were still under construction. We serve the neighborhood as part of our Louisville plumbing service.

Rebuilding facts from the City

The City of Louisville says 532 of the 550 homes destroyed in the Marshall Fire have been rebuilt or have started rebuilding, as of August 2026. That figure covers the whole city and not just this neighborhood.

The City also says tap fees are not assessed for rebuilding homes affected by the fire, as long as the fixture count stays under a set threshold. Ask the City's Building Safety division at (303) 335-4584 about permits and what applies to your plans.

A short checklist for a rebuilt or newer home

Find your main water shutoff and show everyone in the house where it is. Find the shutoff valves under sinks and behind toilets, and turn each one gently once to make sure it moves.

Keep the paperwork for your water heater and fixtures, including who installed them. If something fails early, that record tells you where to start. A plumber can tell you whether a problem is a defect, a wear item or a setting, but only after looking at it.

Getting ready for the call

Find your main water shutoff before you need it. In most houses it is near where the water line enters, often in a utility area or basement. Turn it by hand once so you know it moves. If a fixture is leaking, close its own valve first and leave the main alone.

Write down what you noticed and when it started. A slow drain, a drop in hot water or a damp patch each point to a different cause. Share any recent work done on the house too, because that helps narrow things down quickly.
