A neighborhood of larger homes beside open space

The City of Louisville's Comprehensive Plan existing conditions appendix says the Davidson Mesa neighborhood is named for Davidson Mesa Open Space and consists of large single-family residential homes and local churches. The City's map also shows the open space itself.

We serve Davidson Mesa as part of our Louisville plumbing service. A larger house tends to have more bathrooms and more places for water to go, so the services below lean toward hot water and hidden leaks.

Hot water in a bigger house

A single water heater has to serve every bathroom, the kitchen and the laundry. If the hot water runs out when two showers and a dishwasher run together, the unit may be too small, or it may have failed. A plumber can tell which, and can explain the choice between another tank and a tankless unit.

The Colorado Geological Survey calls expansive soil the state's most significant geologic hazard and says swelling can disrupt pipelines and sewer lines. That is a statewide finding, not a claim about this neighborhood. If you see cracked basement floors or doors that stick, mention it when you call.

Preparing for a visit to a larger home

In a larger house, a problem in one bathroom can be far from the shutoff valve you need. Learn where the main valve is and where each water heater sits, since some homes have more than one. Label them with tape so anyone can find them quickly.

When you call, say which taps, showers or rooms are affected. If every fixture is weak, the issue is likely upstream, and if only one is, the cause is local. That detail points us toward the right page and the right tools before we leave.
