Before We Arrive: what to do in Lafayette

These steps make the visit faster and limit damage.

Find the main shutoff

Know where the water enters your house. Closing it stops most active leaks at once.

Stop using water

If drains are slow or backing up, hold off on showers, laundry and dishes until we look.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the problem so we can reach the fixture.

Note what you saw

Tell us which fixtures are affected, when it started and anything you tried.

Lafayette's older core and what it means for pipes

Lafayette was founded in 1888, and coal mining shaped the town until the Black Diamond mine closed in 1956. Old Town Lafayette, around Public Road and the Chester Street Festival Plaza, includes older buildings, and an older building may have older pipe.

We cannot say how old the plumbing in a given house is until we see it. If yours has galvanized supply lines or clay sewer tile, a camera inspection can show what condition the line is in. For questions about water quality or hardness in Lafayette, ask City of Lafayette utilities.
