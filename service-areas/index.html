Lafayette

Lafayette is the next town east, with about 30,411 residents at the 2020 census. It grew up around coal, and the Black Diamond mine there closed in 1956. Calls from Old Town and the neighborhoods near Waneka Lake Park are common, and the town page lists what to do before we arrive.

Our Lafayette page also explains where to ask about local water supply questions.

Superior

Superior has about 13,094 residents and treats its own water at a plant south of Original Town. The Marshall Fire of 2021 took 398 structures in the town, so many homes are rebuilt. We work on both newer rebuilds and the homes from the 1990s.

Details for the neighborhoods, the water and the permit questions are on our Superior page.

Broomfield

Broomfield is a city and county of 74,112 residents, with homes built from the 1970s through the 2010s. Water hardness varies across its system, which affects scale in heaters and fixtures.

Our Broomfield page sorts neighborhoods by building era and links the services that fit each.

Louisville

Louisville is home. Settled in 1877 around the Welch Mine and incorporated in 1882, it has about 21,226 residents and neighborhoods that run from Old Town to Steel Ranch and Centennial Valley.

Most of what this site covers applies here first, so start with the services list and the homepage.

What to have ready when you call

Give us your street address, the town and what is happening. Tell us whether water is spreading, whether you closed a valve and whether anyone is hurt or in danger. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 first.

For a water heater, the brand, the age and the fuel help. For a drain, tell us which fixtures are slow or backing up.

ZIP codes, plainly

Louisville uses 80027, with 80028 also assigned to the city. Superior has no ZIP code of its own and shares 80027 with Louisville, though parts of town also use 80020 and 80021. Lafayette is 80026, and Broomfield uses 80020, 80021 and 80023.

A ZIP code is a mail route and not a boundary line, so call with your street address and we confirm before we book.

Population figures here follow the 2020 U.S. Census, according to its published counts. Water and permit questions go to the offices of each town.
