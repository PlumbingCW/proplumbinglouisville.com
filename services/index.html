Every plumbing service we offer in Louisville

Louisville Pro Plumbing handles the plumbing problems that cannot wait and the projects that can. The groups below separate emergency repairs from planned work, and each page explains the process, the local details and what affects the price.

Not sure which page fits? Call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. Call to ask about timing.

How to choose the right page

Start with the symptom. Water or sewage coming up a drain belongs with the drain and sewer pages. A tank with no hot water belongs with the water heater pages. A wet spot you cannot explain belongs with leak detection.

If you can see the water and it is spreading, close the main valve first and call second. Our burst pipe page walks through the steps.

Emergency work and planned work

Emergency pages carry steps you can take before a plumber arrives, because the first ten minutes decide how much damage you get. Planned pages, such as installation and repiping, explain permits, sequencing and what affects the price.

Both kinds of work come with a clear price before work starts. The city's Building Safety division lists permits for standalone plumbing work and water heater replacement, and we handle that paperwork when a job needs it.
