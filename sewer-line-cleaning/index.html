Main sewer line cleaning in plain terms

Sewer line cleaning clears the pipe that runs from the house to the connection with the City of Louisville main. A typical visit uses a large cable machine through the cleanout, followed by a camera run to confirm the pipe is open and to spot what caused the blockage.

Because the line serves the whole house, a blockage shows up as several symptoms at once. A toilet that gurgles when the shower drains, a tub that bubbles, or a floor drain that fills during laundry all point downstream of every fixture.

When the same line clogs again, a camera inspection shows the cause, and a damaged pipe leads to sewer line repair. We answer these calls in Superior too.

What to do first when sewage is coming up

This is the situation the 24/7 line exists for. Wastewater in living space needs fast action, and a few steps cut the damage.

  • Stop using water in the house until the line is open.
  • Do not run the washer or dishwasher. They discharge straight into the blocked line.
  • Move electronics and stored boxes off the basement floor if it is safe to do so.
  • Wear boots and gloves if you must enter the area and wash thoroughly afterward.
  • Call the emergency line and describe where the water is coming up so the crew arrives with the right equipment.

Where main line blockages come from

Louisville homes see a handful of repeat causes.

Tree roots

Mature trees in older neighborhoods send fine roots into joints. They enter small and fill the pipe over years.

Wipes and paper products

Products labeled flushable often do not break down, and they catch on rough spots.

Grease and kitchen waste

Fat travels as a liquid and solidifies further down, especially in cooler pipe sections.

A bellied or offset pipe

Shifting soil can leave a low spot that holds water and collects debris. Cleaning gives temporary relief only.

The soil factor

The Colorado Geological Survey calls expansive soil the state's most significant geologic hazard and notes that swelling and shrinking can disrupt pipelines and sewer lines. Much of the Front Range sits on clay-rich soil, so a sewer line that has drifted out of grade is a real possibility in Louisville.

That is why a camera follow-up matters after cleaning. If the footage shows standing water in a section of pipe, the cleaning was only a patch, and a sewer line repair estimate is the next step.

If the blockage is in the city main

Louisville's detail sheets place the sewer service line on the property owner and the main in the street under the City. If your neighbors are backing up as well, the blockage may be in the main. Call Louisville Public Works while you wait for us, since they handle the main.

We can usually tell from the camera which side of the connection holds the blockage, and we will say so plainly.
