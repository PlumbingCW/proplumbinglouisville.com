Toilet problems that cannot wait

Toilet repair covers clogs, overflows, running tanks, weak flushes, leaks at the base and loose or cracked bowls. Most are fixed in one visit with parts on the truck.

The emergency line is open 24/7. We tell you before work starts whether the toilet is worth repairing or ready to replace.

A toilet that keeps clogging may be a drain cleaning problem further down, and a constant hiss may be a hidden leak. We repair toilets in Lafayette homes as well. We also serve homes in the Lake Park neighborhood.

Toilet about to overflow? Do this

A bowl rising toward the rim is a flood in progress.

  • Take the tank lid off and lift the flapper, or close the float, to stop more water entering the bowl.
  • Turn the supply valve behind the toilet clockwise until it stops.
  • Do not flush again, and do not run water elsewhere if more than one drain is slow.
  • Use a flange plunger, not a cup plunger, for a single clogged toilet.
  • If other drains gurgle or back up too, the problem is further down the line. Call us.

Small habits that prevent most toilet calls

Flush only paper and waste. Wipes sold as flushable do not break down like paper and are a top cause of clogs. Keep a flange plunger nearby, because it seals in the toilet drain where a flat cup plunger does not.

Check the tank once a year. Drop a little food coloring in it and wait. If the color reaches the bowl without a flush, the flapper is leaking and you are paying for water that never gets used.

Children, older homes and special cases

Toys and small objects are a frequent cause of a toilet that suddenly refuses to flush. Do not keep flushing. Shut the valve and call, because a plunger can push a hard object deeper.

Older toilets can use far more water per flush than current models. If yours is decades old and needs repeated repairs, replacement may cost less over the next several years.

What the symptom usually means

Runs after flushing

A worn flapper, a bad fill valve or a chain that is the wrong length. It wastes water all day.

Weak flush

Partial clog, blocked rim jets or a low water level in the tank.

Water at the base

A failed wax ring or loose bolts. Do not ignore it, because it soaks the subfloor.

Rocking bowl

Loose closet bolts or a damaged flange.

When one toilet is really a main line problem

If the toilet clears and then backs up again, or if a basement shower gurgles when you flush, the blockage is probably in the sewer line. The City of Louisville's construction detail sheets treat the sewer service line as the property owner's responsibility, including inspection and repair.

We can clear the line, and a camera shows what is causing it. See sewer line cleaning and sewer inspection for the next step.
