Replacing a failed water heater the same day it matters

Water heater replacement removes the old tank, installs a new one and connects water, gas or electric, venting, and the safety valve. When a tank fails at 6 a.m. in winter, the question is how fast a safe replacement can happen.

Our 24/7 line takes failed-tank calls at all hours. We give a clear price before work starts and take the old unit with us.

A planned swap follows water heater installation steps, and a failed part may only need water heater repair. We serve Lafayette and nearby towns with the same crews. Larger homes in Davidson Mesa are part of our service area.

When a tank fails: what to do right away

A tank that has split can release the full volume of the heater within minutes. Act first, then call.

  • Close the cold water supply valve at the heater, then open a hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure.
  • Turn the gas control to off or switch the breaker, depending on the type of heater.
  • Contain the water with towels or a wet vacuum and lift items off the floor.
  • Photograph the unit, the label and the water for your insurer.
  • Call us with the tank size printed on the label so we can have the right unit ready.

Louisville permit and code, in plain terms

The City of Louisville lists replacing a water heater as work that needs a permit. Building Safety is at City Hall, 749 Main Street, open Monday through Thursday from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. and Friday by appointment, and its phone number is (303) 335-4584.

The City also lists the 2024 International Plumbing Code among its adopted codes, effective December 6, 2025. Details such as the relief valve discharge, expansion control and venting follow that adopted code, and the inspector confirms them. A licensed installer should handle the permit and the inspection so you are not left with an unpermitted heater when you sell.

Sizing the new tank for your household

A tank that is too small runs out in the middle of a shower. One that is too large wastes energy keeping water hot. We look at the number of bathrooms, the habits of the household and any big soaking tub before choosing a size.

Louisville homes range from older Old Town houses to larger newer subdivisions, and the right tank is not the same across them. If you are rebuilding after the Marshall Fire, we can size for the plans rather than the old house.
