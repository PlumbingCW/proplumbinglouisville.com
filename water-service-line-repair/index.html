What the water service line is

The water service line is the buried pipe that brings water from the main under the street to your home. It passes the meter and the curb stop, then runs underground to the house.

A failure shows up as a wet or sunken patch in the yard, reduced pressure, a spinning meter with every tap closed or a sudden jump in the bill. The 24/7 line is open.

To confirm the leak is on the buried line, start with leak detection. A burst inside the house is burst pipe repair, and a failing line may justify repiping inside too. We serve Lafayette owners as well.

Who owns which part in Louisville

The City of Louisville's construction detail sheet splits the water service into a property-owner portion and a city-owned portion, with a curb stop between them. The owner is responsible for the pipe from the curb stop to the house. The City handles its own portion.

Before we dig, we confirm where the leak is. If it is on the City's side, we tell you and you call the City. If it is on yours, we repair it. This avoids paying for work that is not yours.

Signs the line is failing before it breaks

A service line rarely fails without warning. Pressure that drops at the same time in every fixture, a meter that creeps with everything closed and a patch of grass that stays greener than the rest are all clues.

Catching a small leak early costs less than a washout. If the line is old, ask about replacement while the ground is open rather than patching the same pipe again.

What to expect during the repair

After the locate, the crew exposes the line, cuts out the failed section and makes the repair. Water will be off for part of the day, and we tell you how long before we start.

Restoration of the dig is part of the plan. Lawn and concrete are different scopes, so ask what is included before the work begins.

Suspect the service line? Do this

Water escaping underground can undermine a driveway, a walk or a slab.

  • Close the main shutoff inside the house and watch the meter. If it still moves, the leak is before your valve.
  • Look for soft ground, pooling water or a patch of green grass in the dry months.
  • Do not dig yourself. Gas, electric and communication lines also run underground.
  • Call 811 before any digging so lines can be marked.
  • Call us and tell us where the water appears.

Lead service lines and Louisville's inventory

The City of Louisville's 2026 water quality report, covering 2025, says the City is still inventorying service lines for lead, with about 1,000 customers having self-reported. For questions about your service line material, the report lists 303-335-4741.

If we expose your line during a repair, we tell you what material we see. Older homes may have different line materials from newer ones, and replacement is a good time to upgrade to modern pipe.
